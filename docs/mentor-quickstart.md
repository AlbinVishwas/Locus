# Locus: a quick start

Locus lets you upload PDF documents and find any word or phrase inside them,
with the exact page it appears on. It runs on Albin's laptop and you use it
through a web link. Nothing here needs any installing.

## 1. Open the link and sign in

1. Open **https://local-chat-one-ivory.vercel.app** in any web browser.
2. Type your **username** and **password** (Albin has sent them to you) and press
   **Continue**.

If it says "Wrong username or password", check for extra spaces and try again.
(The old shared passcode is not used any more.) If the page won't load, the
laptop may be asleep or offline: ask Albin.

## 2. Start a session and upload PDFs

A **session** is a set of documents you search together.

1. You start in a new, empty session. (**New session** in the left-hand list
   makes another.)
2. Press **Upload documents**, or the paperclip, or drag PDF files onto the
   search box.
3. Wait until each file shows its **page count** (for example "97 pages").
   Scanned documents take a little longer because they are read page by page.

## 3. Search for a word or phrase

1. Type what you are looking for, for example `earnest money`, and press **Enter**.
2. The card shows how many pages and documents contain it, then each page with
   the phrase **highlighted** and the page number. Press **Show more** for more
   pages.
3. If you put the phrase in quotes inside a longer sentence, only the quoted
   part is searched.
4. If nothing matches exactly, Locus may offer matches that differ only in
   punctuation (for example `26-2080` against `26/2080`): press that button to
   see them.

To remove a file, use the small remove button next to it. Deleting a session
deletes its files.

## 4. Check that your files are private

Locus is built so that **each person sees only the files they uploaded
themselves**. To see this for yourself, you have a second account, **demo-user**
(Albin has sent you its password too).

1. In your own account (`omkar`), upload a document and search for a word in it.
   Note the result.
2. Press **Log out** at the bottom of the left-hand panel.
3. Sign in as **demo-user**. You start in an empty session: it has no documents,
   and searching for the same word finds nothing. Your document is not visible
   anywhere.
4. Upload a different document as demo-user, search for it, then log out and sign
   back in as `omkar`: that document is not there either.

Each person's sessions are also kept apart in the left-hand list. Only the
administrator (Albin) can see an overview of who uploaded what and who searched
for what; ordinary accounts can't see that or each other's work.

Also worth knowing: **searches are logged for the administrator**, and the
sign-in page says so.

## 5. Change your password

Press **Change password** in the left-hand panel, enter your current password and
a new one (at least 8 characters) twice, and save. You stay signed in here and
are signed out on any other device.

## 6. Finishing up

Press **Log out** when you are done. Before you leave, you can delete your test
sessions (point at a session in the left-hand list, press its small delete button, then confirm) so no documents are left
behind.
